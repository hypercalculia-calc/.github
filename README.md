# Hypercalculia
A private, offline-first intelligent calculator designed for real life.

Hypercalculia is not a normal calculator.  
It is a **general-purpose math and planning engine** for:

- homesteading  
- engineering basics  
- carpentry + construction  
- cisterns, pumps, and water systems  
- agriculture + animals  
- greenhouse + HVAC  
- baking + food science  
- inventory + rationing  
- geometry + land planning  
- teaching math at any level  

Everything runs offline.  
Nothing is sent to the cloud.  
All results are transparent and deterministic.

## Philosophy

Hypercalculia is built for **real people doing real projects**:

- building sheds, barns, greenhouses  
- sizing pumps and cisterns  
- running small farms  
- cooking and baking  
- designing water systems  
- planning gardens  
- managing inventory  

It is intentionally:

- offline  
- private  
- fast  
- understandable  
- extensible  

No AI, no cloud, no hidden logic.
